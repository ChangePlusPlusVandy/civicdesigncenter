2 · One action, end to end

The action round trip

What happens when a team device places a bid, a building, or a gift. The walking skeleton (#19) proves this path with a single “gift 1 wood” action; #25 hardens it.

Full state, never diffs

Every change and every reconnect sends each audience its complete projection plus version. Clients drop anything older than what they hold.

Safe retries

clientActionId is de-duplicated per participant, so a resend after a dropped ack does nothing twice.

Safe restarts

One server, no Redis. Active games rehydrate from their latest snapshot on boot.

Hidden information

Sealed bids only appear in the owning team’s projection until the auction resolves.
